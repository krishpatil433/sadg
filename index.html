Install SADG on iPhone & iPad

Apple Safari doesn't allow one-tap web installations, but you can easily add SADG to your home screen:

1. Tap the Share button in Safari's bottom toolbar.
2. Scroll down in the action sheet and select Add to Home Screen.
3. Tap Add in the top right corner. Enjoy your cloned space!
